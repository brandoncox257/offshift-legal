# Delete your Offshift account

This page is for **Offshift – Roster & Shift Planner**. You only have an Offshift account if you signed in (with Google or an email code). Without one, we hold nothing about you, and uninstalling the app removes everything on your phone.

## Option 1: in the app

1. Open Offshift and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

If you have an active Offshift Pro subscription, the app warns you first and links to your Google Play subscriptions page. **Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.** Cancel it in Google Play (Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Offshift), or you may continue to be charged.

## Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email support@offshift.info from the email address on your account (for Google sign-in, the Gmail address you used). Put "Delete my Offshift account" in the subject. We'll confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in: email address, and any Google profile name and picture.
- Your profile: plan, limits and free-import count.
- Your AI usage log.
- Your subscription status record on our server, if you had one.

## What is kept

- **A one-way hash of your email address** and the number of free photo imports used. It is a scrambled code that we can't turn back into your email. We keep it so that deleting and re-creating an account doesn't hand out three more free photo imports. You can ask us to remove it by emailing us; the allowance would then reset.
- **Purchase records** held by Google Play and RevenueCat, if you bought Pro, as payments and tax rules require. We don't control these.
- Server logs of codes, costs and timings (no content), which expire under our hosting provider's retention.

## What is not affected

Your roster, events, diary and settings live **only on your phone**, so deleting your account does not remove them. To remove them, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

More detail is in the [privacy policy](privacy-policy).
